The wall, redesigned: a quieter day, one voice, both orientations

Every board below is drawn at the iPad's real 1366×1024 size and scaled to fit, using the wall's own palette from wall.css, the app's Besley / Schibsted Grotesk / Spline Sans Mono files, and the real member colors. The sample day is the one from the approved prototype: Saturday, 3:15 pm.

  • Module controls move to an Arrange mode in the display menu.
  • Owners show as initial avatars everywhere (the boards below use them).
  • Always on screen: next event (highlighted), Due today, dinner tonight. The daypart forecast moves one tap away.

Round 5 · Question 21

Front page, readable from across the room

The last Front page set about 45 pieces of text at counter size. These three follow one rule: anything meant to be read from across the kitchen is at least 34 px, Next is 72–84 px, and there are only a handful of such things. Detail is either cut or set apart at counter size. View each at the size you'd stand from the iPad: the small text is meant to disappear.

R1 · Pared front page

A 210 px clock, the day, and one weather phrase. Next as an 80 px headline, then tonight's two events at 34 px. The bottom shows only what's left to do (4 items at 28 px; done items drop off) and tomorrow. The rest of the week is one tap away. It holds 12 pieces of text, down from about 45.

R2 · Poster bands

No columns at all: one column of labeled bands (Next, Tonight, To do, Tomorrow), each a single idea in big type. To-dos become large tappable pills. Tomorrow sits in the tinted module slot at the foot. It reads like a poster, top to bottom.

R3 · Room above, counter below

The screen is split by reading distance. The top 60% holds only room-scale things: clock, day, weather, Next, tonight. The bottom 40% is a tinted counter zone at normal size for when you're standing at the wall: the full Due today checklist and the week. Nothing in the counter zone competes, because it's small and on a quieter ground.

Question 8

A busy day: shrink to fit, or cap the list?

The same Saturday with ten events, six of them still ahead.

Shrink, then capRecommended

useWallFit shrinks Later today down to its floor (0.8). Only past that does it end in "+N more today", which opens Day view. "Next" and Due today never shrink. On a quiet day the same rule grows the type instead.

Fixed cap at 3

Always show the next event plus three, at full size, and tap for the rest. Predictable, but it hides events on a day when there's room for them.

Today's Week screenAs shipped

What's cluttering it

  • 1Four big-number units fight in the top bar. Clock, current temp, H/L, and three daypart temps are all "big serif number + small caption", so the eye has no first stop. There are five temperatures on screen.
  • 2The date is said twice. "Saturday October 3" in the top bar, then "Today Saturday" as the column head.
  • 3A name under every event. 21 dots and 21 name labels on one screen. The dot already carries the person; the name doubles the line count in Today.
  • 4Editing chrome on the resting screen. Switch, ×, and the dashed Add module box are set up once and then visible all day.
  • 5Past events keep full size. Three finished events sit at the top of Today and push what's next halfway down.
  • 6Fourteen days at equal weight. Wednesday's six lines get as much room as tomorrow's two, so the panel reads as a wall of rows.
  • 7No type scale. This one screen uses 15 sizes (13–72 px). The steps are too close for a reader across the kitchen to tell what's primary.

What's worth keeping

  • ✓The warm paper palette and Besley moments. It looks like LifeBalance, not a generic smart display.
  • ✓The now-line in Today. The best idea on the wall: it splits done from next without any chrome.
  • ✓Consistent member colors across Week, Day blocks, and Month.
  • ✓Tap-to-check rows with a 10 s Undo. Big circular targets, no confirmation dialogs.
  • ✓Shopping by store in two columns. Already uncluttered: one voice for headings, one for items, generous rows. It's the benchmark the other screens should match.
  • ✓Type that fits its box (useWallFit): a quiet day reads bigger, a busy one never clips.
