## 2025-05-20 - Predictive Command Center **Insight:** Users must navigate deep into menus or modals to perform frequent actions (log expense, add todo), creating unnecessary friction. **Concept:** A global "Command Palette" (Cmd+K) that uses AI to predict intent (time-of-day suggestions) and enables one-shot natural language actions (e.g., "Lunch $20" -> Pre-filled Entry), effectively turning the app into a proactive assistant rather than a passive tool.
