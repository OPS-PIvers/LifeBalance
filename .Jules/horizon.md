## 2026-01-23 - Magic Command Center
**Insight:** Users face high friction when performing simple actions (logging expenses, adding items) because they must navigate deep into specific tabs ("Budget", "Meals").
**Concept:** A "Magic Command Bar" on the Dashboard that accepts natural language (text/voice) to predict and execute actions instantly (e.g., "Spent $50 on gas" -> auto-creates transaction), reducing time-to-action by 90%.
