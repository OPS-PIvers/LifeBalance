## 2025-05-22 - Hidden History **Discovery:** The `todos` array from `useHousehold` contains ALL tasks (active & completed), but the UI strictly filters out `isCompleted: true` items. **Opportunity:** Expose a "Completed" view to let users track achievements, restore accidentally completed tasks, and delete old history, with zero backend changes.
## 2025-05-23 - Batch Reschedule **Discovery:** The `Section` component structure in `ToDosPage` allows easy injection of new action buttons, and `updateToDo` context method supports partial updates. **Opportunity:** Implemented "Batch Reschedule" and "Move to Tomorrow" using existing context methods, accelerating task management without new backend logic.
## 2025-05-24 - Unbudgeted Visibility **Discovery:** Transactions with no category or categories without matching buckets were silently filtered out of the Budget Buckets view. **Opportunity:** Created a virtual "Unbudgeted & Other" bucket to catch these orphaned transactions, alerting users to hidden spending and prompting categorization.
## 2026-02-09 - Smart Cookbook **Discovery:** Users with growing meal collections struggled to find specific recipes in the simple "Previous Meals" list. **Opportunity:** Transformed the list into a Smart Cookbook with search, tag filtering, and sorting.
## 2026-02-11 - Daily Habits Dashboard **Discovery:** The Dashboard was missing habit tracking visibility, forcing navigation. **Opportunity:** Implemented `DailyHabitsWidget` reusing `useHousehold` and `toggleHabit` to gamify the home screen.
## 2025-05-25 - Copy Meal Plan **Discovery:** Meal planning often involves sharing the week’s schedule with family members or checking it on another device without opening the app. **Opportunity:** Implemented a "Copy to Clipboard" feature in the MealPlanTab to convert the week’s schedule into a readable text format.
## 2025-05-25 - Duplicate Meal **Discovery:** When dragging or reusing meals, users had to recreate them entirely or search through past items to add another instance of a meal. **Opportunity:** Implemented a Duplicate and Move to Tomorrow function directly on the Meal Plan items.
